# Competitor Categories

## Enterprise ERP

Examples

- SAP
- Oracle
- Microsoft Dynamics

Purpose

Complete business management.

Strength

Very powerful.

Weakness

Complex.

Expensive.

Long implementation.

Not designed for SMEs.

---

## AP Automation

Examples

- Tipalti
- Stampli
- Yooz
- AvidXchange

Purpose

Automate invoice processing.

Strength

Good OCR.

Strong approval workflows.

Weakness

Focused on invoices rather than daily finance work.

---

## Spend Management

Examples

- Ramp
- Airbase
- Brex

Purpose

Control company spending.

Strength

Excellent employee experience.

Modern interfaces.

Weakness

Invoice processing is not the primary focus.

---

## Accounting Software

Examples

- QuickBooks
- Xero
- Zoho Books
- Tally

Purpose

Accounting and bookkeeping.

Strength

General accounting.

Weakness

Limited AP workflow.

# Decision Rules

Every new feature must strengthen one of these:

- Simplicity
- Workflow
- Visibility
- Speed
- Accuracy

If it weakens any of them, reject it.
