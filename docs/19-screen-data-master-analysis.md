# Master Screen & Data Architecture Analysis

This document provides the definitive data inventory and dependency map for the Avarta AP Workspace across all screens.

---

## 1. Master Screen Catalog & Baseline Purpose

| # | Screen Name | Route URL | Purpose (Who Uses It & What It Is For) |
|---|-------------|-----------|----------------------------------------|
| 1 | **Login** | `/login` | Authenticates enterprise users across 5 canonical roles and establishes secure JWT session with tenant isolation. |
| 2 | **Dashboard / Overview** | `/overview` | AP mission control used by Finance Managers to monitor total payables, triage urgent overdue invoices, and track 30-day cash outflow. |
| 3 | **Inbox / Upload** | `/inbox` | Intake workstation used by Finance Executives to ingest multi-source invoices (drag-and-drop, email webhook, portal) and review OCR extraction. |
| 4 | **Invoice List** | `/invoices` | Central register used by all AP staff to filter, search, sort, and batch-triage invoices across all 10 linear workflow statuses. |
| 5 | **Invoice Detail** | `/invoices/:id` | Working view used by AP clerks and managers to verify OCR line items, evaluate 3-way match against POs/GRNs, and inspect the immutable audit trail. |
| 6 | **Exceptions Queue** | `/exceptions` | Operational triage queue used by Finance Executives and Managers to resolve 14 canonical AP discrepancies (price/quantity variances, invalid GST, fraud risk). |
| 7 | **Approvals Queue** | `/approvals` | Delegation-of-authority decision queue used by Approvers and Managers to review and approve/reject invoices within tiered financial thresholds. |
| 8 | **Disbursement & Payments** | `/payments` | Liquidity hub used by Finance Managers to execute scheduled disbursements, manage Host-to-Host banking accounts, and release batch payments. |
| 9 | **Suppliers / Vendors** | `/suppliers` | Master vendor catalog used by AP staff to maintain supplier profiles, verify statutory GSTIN status, bank details, and monitor credit balances. |
| 10 | **Purchase Orders** | `/purchase-orders` | PO liability tracker used by procurement and AP to track committed funds, line-item deliveries, net GRN quantities, and receiving caps. |
| 11 | **Reports & Analytics** | `/reports` | Executive intelligence hub used by Finance Managers and CFOs to analyze AP aging buckets, cycle times, cash forecasts, and ROI savings. |
| 12 | **Archive** | `/archive` | Immutable compliance repository used by auditors and CFOs to inspect Section 44AA tax-retained invoices with SHA-256 digital certificates. |
| 13 | **Settings** | `/settings` | Administrative control center used by Administrators to configure approval tiers, financial defaults, ERP gateways, and view security audit logs. |
| 14 | **User Management** | `/settings?tab=users` | Tenant identity panel used by Administrators to inspect team roles, permissions, active sessions, and execute one-click demo data resets. |
| 15 | **System Notifications** | `/notifications` | Operational notification tray used by all users to view high-priority workflow pings, threshold escalations, and assignment requests. |
| 16 | **User Profile** | `/profile` | Personal account settings used by all users to view assigned roles, security credentials, session tokens, and theme preferences. |
